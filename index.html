About

Sold it, then built the motion for it.

Antaeus sells complex software to large organizations, where deals involve many stakeholders, security review and a long road to signature.

He has more than eleven years as an enterprise account executive, including two founding AE roles: for Remote's CM product and at Fiverr Enterprise. Earlier he sold at Textio, first on the East territory from Seattle and then as a senior AE in Chicago, and co-founded RiverCookie.

Today he works two ways: as a hands-on seller for SaaS and AI companies, and as a consultant who uses MEDDIC to keep long deal cycles honest.
